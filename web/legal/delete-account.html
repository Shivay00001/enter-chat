Permanently delete your EnterChat account and all associated data.

⚠️ This action is permanent and cannot be undone

Deleting your account will permanently remove:

  • Your profile information (name, photo, status)
  • All your messages from our servers
  • Your group memberships and community subscriptions
  • Your call history
  • All media files you've uploaded
  • Your device registrations and sessions

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: From the App (Recommended)

  • Open EnterChat

    Launch the app and make sure you're logged in.

  • Go to Settings

    Tap the Settings tab (gear icon) at the bottom.

  • Tap "Account"

    Scroll down to the Account section.

  • Tap "Delete Account"

    Tap the red "Delete Account" button.

  • Confirm with OTP

    For security, you'll receive a verification code. Enter it to confirm deletion.

  • Account deleted

    Your account and all data will be permanently deleted within 90 days.

Option 2: Via Email

If you cannot access the app, send an email to delete@enterchat.app with:

  • Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
  • The phone number or email associated with your account
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

We will verify your identity and process the deletion within 7 business days.

Data Deletion Timeline

  • Immediately — Account is deactivated, you're logged out
  • Within 24 hours — Profile removed from search and contacts
  • Within 30 days — Messages deleted from our servers
  • Within 90 days — All data permanently erased from backups

Note: Messages you sent to other users may still exist on their devices. We cannot delete data from other users' devices.

Questions?

Contact us at privacy@enterchat.app for any questions about account deletion.
